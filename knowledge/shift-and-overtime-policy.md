# Shift and overtime policy

## Shifts
The plant runs one shift per day, from 06:00 to 22:00, with a 30 minute break at about 12:00 and a 15 minute
break at about 17:30. Machines are not scheduled outside the shift.

## Overtime
Overtime is not scheduled by the planner. It needs the production manager's approval at least 4 hours before
the shift ends, and is limited to 2 hours per day. Overtime requires at least 2 operators per running machine.

## Weekends
There is no weekend production unless the plant director authorises it in writing.
