# Incident report: power dip, September 2025

## What happened
A voltage dip at 14:20 stopped all four machines at once. Power was back after 6 minutes.

## Restart
Each machine needs a 20 minute warm-up and a spindle check after a power loss. Machines were restarted one at a
time, M1 first, so the whole shop was producing again after 85 minutes. Operations in progress at 14:20 all
restarted from the beginning.

## Lessons
After a shop-wide stop, restart in the order M1, M2, M3, M4. Expect about 85 minutes before the shop is back
to full output, not the 6 minutes of the dip itself.
