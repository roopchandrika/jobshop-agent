# SOP: rush orders

## When a rush order is allowed
A rush order is an order the customer needs sooner than the standard lead time. Only the production manager
can approve a rush order. A rush order gets priority 5 only when a contract penalty applies to lateness;
otherwise it gets priority 4.

## Displacing other work
A rush order may not push back an order of priority 4 or 5 that is already within 60 minutes of its due time
without the production manager's written approval. Orders of priority 3 or lower may be pushed back.

## Rush order limit
At most 2 rush orders may be accepted per shift. A third needs the plant director's approval.
