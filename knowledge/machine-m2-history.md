# Machine M2: maintenance history

## Spindle bearing
The M2 spindle bearing has failed three times in two years, in March 2024, February 2025 and October 2025. Each
repair took between 2 and 4 hours. The bearing is now replaced every 6 months, next due in April 2026.

## Vibration warning
A rising vibration reading above 4.5 mm/s on M2 is the early sign of bearing wear. When it is seen, maintenance
should be asked to inspect M2 at the next break.

## Capabilities
M2 can mill, drill and grind. It is the only machine other than M3 and M4 that can grind.
