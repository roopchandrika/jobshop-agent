# Incident report: M3 coolant pump failure, November 2025

## What happened
On a Tuesday morning the coolant pump on M3 failed during a drilling operation. M3 was out of service for 3
hours and 10 minutes. The operation that was running had to restart from the beginning.

## What was done
Drilling work was moved to M1 and M2, which can also drill. Grinding work normally done on M3 waited for M2 to
become free. Two orders finished late, by 25 and 40 minutes.

## Lessons
Keep a spare pump on site (done in December 2025). Move drilling to M1 first, because M2 is the only other
machine that can grind, and grinding capacity is the tightest.
